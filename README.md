Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261126](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261126): sum up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026 in one image.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×576) is a newspaper front page, "The World in One Day", with six stories that defined the day — trade, energy, Gaza, Sudan, space and India — each with a short, sourced summary. The poster is generated by `make_poster.py` (Python + Pillow, no network, deterministic).

## The six stories

1. **Trade** — Trump proposes new tariffs on 14 partners; China faces +20%, up to 40% if the truce fails. (Reuters, 25 Nov)
2. **Energy** — Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, Ras Tanura; satellite images show thick black smoke, Brent jumps past $93. (Reuters, 25 Nov)
3. **Gaza** — Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief Mohammad Abuelouf, his wife and two daughters in Gaza City. (Reuters, 25 Nov)
4. **Sudan** — RSF drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Damazin, Blue Nile state; 20 wounded. (Reuters, 25 Nov)
5. **Space** — Pakistan's first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, docks at China's Tiangong station on Shenzhou-24. (France 24 / Al Jazeera, 25 Nov)
6. **India** — India marks 18 years since the 26/11 Mumbai attacks and celebrates Constitution Day on 26 November. (26 Nov)

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
